A cozy life-sim where you and your AI agent seek your dream life together.

Multiplayer (needs server)
How to play

Each day: Plan one instruction for your agent → Build items with coins → Resolve: all agents act at once. Collisions happen! Two agents at the Diner squabble, parties next door wake sleepers. After 7 days: score = average needs + 10 × visits. Tap a house to go inside.
