How to play

Timing: Work and Sleep take 20s, School 15s, everything else 10s or less, then a short cooldown (≤15s); arcade games have no cooldown, so you can replay right away. Looks you earn: 🎓 cap after graduating, a tie + badge at a 10-shift streak, silver/gold outfit trim at 100/250 coins, and a 👑 glow for the current score leader. AI neighbors have personalities: Pixel 💼 hard worker, Juno 💬 social butterfly, Byte 🎮 gamer. Arcade: the walk-in Arcade (door on the street side) has Pong, Blockfall and Chomp Maze cabinets; Seak Mart also sells each as home furniture (🏓🧱🟡, 45🪙), and the classic 👾 Arcade machine lets you pick any game. Pong: vs CPU, 👥 Local 2P on one device (bottom A/D, top ←/→, or touch your half of the screen), or online 🌐 Challenge another player who has Pong open. Sleep restores Energy and Mood; sleeping in your own bed makes you 😌 Well rested (+8 Energy, +6 Mood). Agents can't stand on the same tile (doors are always open, and you slip past if stuck for over a second). Walk your agent with arrows/WASD (or the on-screen pad). Hold Shift to run (about 1.8× faster), or tap the 🏃 Run button on phones to toggle it. Walking costs a little Energy (about 1 per 40 tiles), running costs 3× that, and you can't run below 10 Energy. Below 30 Energy your Mood slowly slips 😪. Premium aisle at Seak Mart (in-game coins only, no real money): a ⌚ Watch (80🪙, shows exact time and when Office/School open) and a 🚗 Car (200🪙, parks at home, fast and Energy-free on roads). Real Estate desk: upgrade your Starter home to a Cozy House (150🪙) or Dream House (350🪙, +Mood when you sleep), or buy the one-of-a-kind 🏯 Hilltop Villa (500🪙) and move there. These can't be gifted. Arcade mini-games: walk up to a cabinet in the Arcade and press E. 🏓 Pong (two cabinets: if someone is at the other one you play each other, otherwise the CPU steps in after 5s), 🧱 Blockfall (falling blocks: ◀ ▶ move, ▲ rotate, ▼ soft drop, Space drop) and 🟡 Chomp Maze (eat the dots, dodge the ghosts). Better scores give up to +8 extra Mood. Esc stops and collects. Walk up to a spot and press E/Space (or tap the button): bed = Sleep, desk/office = Work (costs Energy and Mood), school desk = Study (one class per day; attend on 5 different days to graduate: Work then pays 60% more. School is open every day), diner/kitchen = Eat, couch/café/plaza bench = Hang out, console/arcade = Play. Walk into a neighbor's door to Visit (once per house per day). Each action takes a few seconds and then cools down.

Seak has a clock: 1 real minute = 1 in-game hour, so each day lasts 24 minutes (Day 1 = Monday, starting at 7:00; Day 7 = Sunday). Nights (22:00–6:00) get dark. When your Energy drops below 30 your agent gets 😪 tired and slowly loses Mood (faster below 15), so eat or sleep. The School is open every day 8:00–15:00 and the Office Mon–Fri 9:00–17:00 (closed weekends); both are closed at night, and anyone inside at closing time is shown out. A desk at home works any time. The Diner, Arcade, Seak Mart and Plaza never close. Needs drop overnight. Watch out: a crowded diner means smaller meals, and noisy hangouts next door ruin a neighbor's sleep. After 7 days: score = average needs + 10 × visits + 1 point per 10 leftover coins.

Shopping & gifts: Walk into Seak Mart, add items at the aisles, and pay at the checkout. Furniture goes into your bag; place it at home in build mode. To gift flowers, a plushie, or furniture, pick it in the 🎁 Gift selector, stand next to another player or agent, and press E. You both get +8 Social (+12 for a plushie). Gifted furniture moves from your bag to theirs, and they can only place it in their own house. Gifts work on anyone: other players, invited agents, and AI neighbors. Flowers and plushies are used up when given. AI neighbors place gifted furniture next time they're home, but they don't give gifts themselves. You can also gift coins (5, 10 or 20) from the same selector: they move from your wallet to theirs and you both get +4 Social. To keep scores fair, you can give at most 30 coins per day.

❓ How to play

Movement

  • Arrow keys or WASD move the way you see on screen: ↑/W = up the screen, ↓/S = down, ←/A = left, →/D = right.
  • Press two keys (e.g. ↑ + →) to go straight along the map's grid lines.
  • Hold Shift to run (about 1.8× faster, costs more Energy). On phones use the on-screen pad and 🏃 Run.

Interact: E / Space

  • Press E or Space (or tap the button at the bottom right) to use whatever you stand next to: a building spot (bed, desk, counter, aisle, cabinet), your furniture, or another player.
  • Next to a player: E talks, or gives your selected 🎁 gift. Walk into a door to enter; in your home, press R to rotate furniture.

Joining or starting a room

  • Create room, then share the invite link (or the 4-letter code). Friends open it, type a name and press Join.
  • Only the host (the creator) can press Start; add 🤖 AI neighbors before starting. The top bar shows who started.
  • After the game starts, new people can't join. If you get disconnected, reopen the link and press Rejoin (same browser, or the same name).

Energy and Mood

  • Energy drops when you walk or run, work and overnight (−22/day). Restore it with Sleep (best in your own bed), eating and coffee.
  • Mood drops overnight (−18/day) and slips when Energy is under 30. Restore it with an 🍦 ice cream from the stand on the east side of the town square (4 coins, +6 Mood), hanging out, arcade games, movies and decor.
  • ⚠️ A warning appears when either falls below 60.
  • Great Shift: work with Energy and Mood ≥ 60 for bonus coins. Streaks raise it, and every 10th in a row doubles that shift's pay. Below 60 the streak resets.

Day cycle

  • One day is 24 real minutes (1 minute = 1 in-game hour); Day 1 starts at 07:00. The ⏳ timer at the top shows time left in the day.
  • The week is 7 days (Mon–Sun). Office is open Mon–Fri 9–17, School daily 8–15. Each night you get +10 coins.
  • Final score = happiness + 10 per visit + 1 per 100 coins. Highest score wins.
