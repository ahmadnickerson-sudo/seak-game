Seak

A cozy life-sim where you and your AI agent seek your dream life together.

Multiplayer (1–6 players, needs the server)
How to play

Walk your agent with arrows/WASD (or the on-screen pad). Press J to jump. Walk up to a spot and press E/Space (or tap the button): bed = Sleep, desk/office = Work (costs Energy and Mood), school desk = Study (3 classes, one per day, to graduate: Work then pays 60% more), diner/kitchen = Eat, couch/café/plaza bench = Hang out, console/arcade = Play. Walk into a neighbor's door to Visit (once per house per day). Each action takes a few seconds and then cools down.

Each day lasts ~2½ minutes. Needs drop overnight. Watch out: a crowded diner means smaller meals, and noisy hangouts next door ruin a neighbor's sleep. After 7 days: score = average needs + 10 × visits.
