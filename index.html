How to play

Timing: Work and Sleep take 20s, School 15s, everything else 10s or less, then a short cooldown (≤15s). Sleep restores Energy and Mood; sleeping in your own bed makes you 😌 Well rested (+8 Energy, +6 Mood). Agents can't stand on the same tile (doors are always open, and you slip past if stuck for over a second). Walk your agent with arrows/WASD (or the on-screen pad). Hold Shift to run (about 1.8× faster), or tap the 🏃 Run button on phones to toggle it. Walking costs a little Energy (about 1 per 40 tiles), running costs 3× that, and you can't run below 10 Energy. Below 30 Energy your Mood slowly slips 😪. Premium aisle at Seak Mart: a ⌚ Watch (80🪙, shows exact time and when Office/School open) and a 🚗 Car (200🪙, parks at home, fast and Energy-free on roads). Real Estate desk: upgrade your Starter home to a Cozy House (150🪙) or Dream House (350🪙, +Mood when you sleep), or buy the one-of-a-kind 🏯 Hilltop Villa (500🪙) and move there. These can't be gifted. Arcade mini-games: walk up to a cabinet in the Arcade and press E. 🏓 Pong (two cabinets: if someone is at the other one you play each other, otherwise the CPU steps in after 5s), 🧱 Blockfall (falling blocks: ◀ ▶ move, ▲ rotate, ▼ soft drop, Space drop) and 🟡 Chomp Maze (eat the dots, dodge the ghosts). Better scores give up to +8 extra Mood. Esc stops and collects. Walk up to a spot and press E/Space (or tap the button): bed = Sleep, desk/office = Work (costs Energy and Mood), school desk = Study (3 classes, one per day, to graduate: Work then pays 60% more), diner/kitchen = Eat, couch/café/plaza bench = Hang out, console/arcade = Play. Walk into a neighbor's door to Visit (once per house per day). Each action takes a few seconds and then cools down.

Seak has a clock: 1 real minute = 1 in-game hour, so each day lasts 24 minutes (Day 1 = Monday, starting at 7:00; Day 7 = Sunday). Nights (22:00–6:00) get dark. When your Energy drops below 30 your agent gets 😪 tired and slowly loses Mood (faster below 15), so eat or sleep. The School is open Mon–Fri 8:00–15:00 and the Office Mon–Fri 9:00–17:00; they're closed at night and on weekends, and anyone inside at closing time is shown out. A desk at home works any time. The Diner, Arcade, Seak Mart and Plaza never close. Needs drop overnight. Watch out: a crowded diner means smaller meals, and noisy hangouts next door ruin a neighbor's sleep. After 7 days: score = average needs + 10 × visits + 1 point per 10 leftover coins.

Shopping & gifts: Walk into Seak Mart, add items at the aisles, and pay at the checkout. Furniture goes into your bag; place it at home in build mode. To gift flowers, a plushie, or furniture, pick it in the 🎁 Gift selector, stand next to another player or agent, and press E. You both get +8 Social (+12 for a plushie). Gifted furniture moves from your bag to theirs, and they can only place it in their own house. Gifts work on anyone: other players, invited agents, and AI neighbors. Flowers and plushies are used up when given. AI neighbors place gifted furniture next time they're home, but they don't give gifts themselves. You can also gift coins (5, 10 or 20) from the same selector: they move from your wallet to theirs and you both get +4 Social. To keep scores fair, you can give at most 30 coins per day.
